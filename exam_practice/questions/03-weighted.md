Measure the circle directly. Give the probability of each color.

## Solution

### Combine matching contributions

The white amplitude is 4 and the black amplitude is 1. The five drawn entries are not five equally likely measurement outcomes.

### Square and normalize

The weights are 4² = 16 and 1² = 1. Their total is 17. Divide each weight by 17.

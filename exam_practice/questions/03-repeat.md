The circle has just been measured **black**. You apply NOT to the **square**, then immediately measure the circle again. What can you predict?

## Solution

### Use the recorded outcome

After the first measurement, all surviving complete patterns contain a black circle.

### Follow which qubit changes

NOT on the square changes only square values. The circle remains black in every surviving term, so measuring it again gives black with certainty.

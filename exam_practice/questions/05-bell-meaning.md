Select **all** correct statements about entanglement and Bell tests.

## Solution

### One setting is insufficient

Two systems can share matching or opposite prearranged values. Correlation alone in one measurement setting does not establish a Bell violation.

### Compare several settings

Bell reasoning tests whether one local instruction model can account for all settings under the stated assumptions. Local results remain random; comparing records needs ordinary communication.

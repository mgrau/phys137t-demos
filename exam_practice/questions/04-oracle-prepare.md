In Money or Tiger, which target-circle preparation makes an oracle NOT leave a **sign** that the selector can detect through interference?

## Solution

### Compare the two H clouds

NOT interchanges white and black. The plus cloud is unchanged. The minus cloud becomes its overall negative.

### Use that sign on selected branches

The oracle flips the circle only on branches selecting a tiger door. Its sign becomes relative between selector branches. H on an initially black target supplies the needed minus cloud.

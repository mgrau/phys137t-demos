Measure the circle. Account for cancellation **before** calculating probabilities.

## Solution

### Combine by complete pattern

White has amplitude 1 + 1 = 2. Black has amplitude 1 − 1 − 1 = −1.

### Use the surviving amplitudes

Square to get weights 4 and 1. Divide each by 5. Counting the original five entries equally would give the wrong probabilities.

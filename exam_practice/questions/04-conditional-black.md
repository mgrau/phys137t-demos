The square is measured **black**. Draw the remaining **two-qubit state**, preserving any relative sign.

## Solution

### Select the black-square terms

The surviving terms are white circle–black square and negative black circle–black square. The white-square terms are discarded.

### Preserve their ratio and sign

Measurement does not erase the relative minus between surviving terms. The square is definite black; the circle is in its minus cloud.

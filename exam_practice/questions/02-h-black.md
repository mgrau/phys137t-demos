Draw the output of H. Include the **relative sign**.

## Solution

### Use the black-input rule

H sends black to positive white and negative black.

### Distinguish relative and overall signs

Only the relative sign matters. White minus black and negative white plus black describe the same state. Two positive terms describe a different state.

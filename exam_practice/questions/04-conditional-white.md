The square is measured **white**. Draw the remaining **two-qubit state**, including the measured square.

## Solution

### Retain only matching terms

Keep the two white-white contributions. Discard the white-black and negative black-black terms because their square is black.

### Remove a common factor

Two copies of the same complete pattern have no relative amplitude to compare. Remove the common factor of two, leaving a definite white circle and white square.

Find both direct measurement probabilities.

## Solution

### Read signed amplitudes

The white amplitude is 3. The black amplitude is −1.

### Square the whole amplitude

The weights are 9 and 1 because (−1)² = 1. Divide by the total weight, 10. A negative amplitude does not make a negative probability.

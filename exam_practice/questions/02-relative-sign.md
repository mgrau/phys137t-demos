Which statement correctly compares these two clouds?

## Solution

### Measure directly

Both have white and black amplitudes of equal magnitude. Squaring gives equal direct color probabilities.

### Apply H before measuring

The plus cloud becomes white. The minus cloud becomes black. Interference makes their different relative signs observable.
